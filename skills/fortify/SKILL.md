# Fortify Skill

**Mission**: Turn validated findings into the strongest practical version of the artifact without unnecessarily expanding its scope.

Use Fortify after sufficient findings are generated by Frame and Break. Rely strictly on the existing findings from Frame and Break. Only initiate new discovery if a critical dependency is undocumented.

**Triggers**: `/fortify <document>` or `/fortify <document> BREAK-REVIEW.md`

---

## The Evidence Contract (Summary)

*   **Evidence Hierarchy**: Prefer laws, regulations, and official standards.
*   **Claim Classification**: Distinguish between Verified, Supported, Inferred, Assumed, and Unknown claims.
*   **Source Fit**: Only apply external frameworks if they meaningfully apply to the artifact and context.
*   **Citation Rule**: Identify sources for recommendations grounded in external standards.

*(For full details, see `references/evidence-contract.md`)*

---

## Process

### Step 1 — Preserve Intent
Restate the agreed objective. Improvements must support that objective. Explicitly propose strategy alternatives to the user if you believe the original strategy is flawed, but maintain the author's intent.

### Step 2 — Resolve Findings
For every Blocker and Major Concern identified in the Break phase, determine whether to:
* Fix
* Mitigate
* Accept
* Escalate
* Defer

Unresolved material choices become explicit decisions for the user to make. Proceed directly with unresolved choices. Only initiate a new interview if a completely new consequential decision emerges.

### Step 3 — Strengthen the Artifact
Where applicable, improve: objectives, scope, sequencing, ownership, responsibilities, dependencies, governance, decision rights, controls, measures, acceptance criteria, contingencies, communications, implementation steps, escalation paths.

### Step 4 — Make Success Observable
Replace vague completion language with observable criteria.
*Weak*: "Users understand the change."
*Stronger*: "Affected users demonstrate the required process during readiness validation and the agreed adoption indicators meet the defined threshold."

Use context-appropriate metrics rather than inventing arbitrary numbers.

### Step 5 — Trace Changes
Every material change should map back to at least one of:
* Original requirement
* Frame finding
* Break finding
* External evidence
* Explicit user decision

### Step 6 — Re-validate
Before finishing, check:
* Original intent remains intact
* Blockers have been addressed or explicitly accepted
* New contradictions were not introduced
* New unsupported claims were not introduced
* Critical dependencies have owners
* Important outcomes have observable success criteria

---

## Output

Produce the following either as direct file modifications or a new proposed markdown file:

### A. Strengthened Artifact
The revised plan, playbook, framework, or document.

### B. Change Ledger
For material changes, record: Change, Reason, Source finding, Evidence, Trade-off.

### C. Residual Risks
Anything deliberately left unresolved.

### D. Validation Matrix
Table format: `Requirement | Evidence | Test | Status`

### E. Final Verdict
Choose: Ready, Ready with conditions, Not ready (explain conditions or blockers).

---

## Completion Criterion

Fortify is complete only when every material recommendation from Break has a disposition and every material change is traceable to a requirement, finding, evidence source, or explicit decision. Check that every step in the artifact has an assigned owner, a clear action, and an observable metric of success.
